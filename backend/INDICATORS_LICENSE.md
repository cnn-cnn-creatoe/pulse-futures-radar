Changes made for this dashboard: Python implementation on a single timeframe of fully closed, continuous candles; separately retained internal and swing `leg(size)` states; delayed pivot confirmation; close-crossing BOS/CHoCH events; high/low-mitigated order blocks; ATR200-based equal high/low tolerance; three-candle FVG with causal cumulative displacement threshold; confirmed swing premium/discount zones. Every zone has a confirmation timestamp. Display history and zone counts are bounded. Parsed inverted order-block bounds are discarded instead of creating an invalid zone. Internal events sharing an established swing level are deduplicated. Confirmed historical pivot levels are classified relative to the current price to expose nearby support and resistance.

The API exposes these same-timeframe results as independent chart layers for the Supertrend line and flips, BOS/CHoCH events, active order blocks, equal highs/lows, active FVGs, confirmed premium/equilibrium/discount zones, confirmed swing points, and current strong/weak swing levels. Human-facing labels are Chinese while the original abbreviations remain in stable `code` fields. A chart object's `time` or `start_time` is the bar on which the result became knowable; its earlier `pivot_time` or `origin_time`, when present, is provenance only. `confirmed_at` is the corresponding closed-bar time. Consumers must not draw a layer before `confirmed_at`.

The original script's multi-timeframe `request.security(... lookahead_on)` calls, retrospective drawing placements, live open-candle calculations, decorative styles, and drawing-object operations are **not** reproduced. FVG is evaluated only after its third candle closes. Premium/discount bounds use confirmed swing extremes rather than extending with unconfirmed swing points. ATR200 warm-up is disclosed; missing ATR200 never fabricates equal-level confirmations. Thus this is a transparent, non-repainting adaptation, **not a bit-for-bit TradingView/LuxAlgo replica**.

The Supertrend implementation follows the separate Pine v4 formula supplied by the user: HL2 source, ATR10, multiplier3, previous-close trailing bands, and crossings of previous bands. Wilder ATR is seeded from an arithmetic mean of true ranges; optional `sma(tr,N)` preserves Pine's missing first previous-close behavior. The supplied Supertrend excerpt had no author/license header; no additional third-party ownership or permission is asserted. EMA is a conventional first-value-seeded exponential moving average.

Technical reference for Pine true-range warm-up semantics: TradingView, [Built-ins](https://www.tradingview.com/pine-script-docs/language/built-ins/). `tr` and `tr(true)` differ when the previous close is unavailable.

Screening and reference trade-plan calculations are new integration logic. They do not place orders, guarantee a winning trade, replace execution checks, or provide a reliable estimate of future returns. Fees/slippage/spread are configurable assumptions; future funding and liquidation effects are not modeled by the indicator module.
